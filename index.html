The proposition

A place of distinction in Wuse II

Class A · Post-industrial character

I-Space is a Class A office building that stands out with its unique character. The project blends a lofty, post-industrial style with high-quality materials and energy-efficient solutions, leading to low operating costs.

The representative, two-storey entrance hall with reception and a comfortable waiting area sets the tone on arrival. Loft-inspired offices sit above it, lit by large tilt windows that illuminate the interior and let fresh air circulate. Benches, pergolas and carefully designed greenery around the building and on the terrace create places for less formal meetings and rest.

Steel & glass
A vertical steel screen wraps the glazed volumes, shading the west face and giving the building its street identity.
Flexible floors
Open space or modular layout on every level; optional single or multiple tenancy per floor.

Interior design concept

Rooted. Refined. Timeless.

Modern high-end curation

The interior is conceived as a modern, high-end curation inspired by the quiet strength and elegance of a tree. The concept draws on the harmony between nature and architecture, translating organic warmth into a sophisticated workplace experience.

Warm ivories, textured mineral walls, natural wood finishes, charcoal accents and greenery balance softness with sophistication. Linear wood ceilings echo the rhythm of branches; stone-inspired finishes read as grounded permanence.

The result
A workspace that feels modern, elevated and deeply intentional — a refined sanctuary where productivity meets calm.
